Research

MSc Thesis — Probability Distribution Theory
King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals · Supervisor: Dr. Emmanuel Afuecheta

Developed The Sine–Fréchet-G Family of Continuous Distributions. Derived moments, quantile and hazard functions, performed maximum-likelihood estimation with Monte Carlo evaluation, and demonstrated superior fit on real datasets.

→ Open the interactive Sine Fréchet Explorer (Shiny app)

Undergraduate Project
Modibbo Adama University · Supervisor: Prof. Sauta S. Abdulkadir

Statistical Properties of the Lomax–Inverse Exponential Distribution and applications to real-life data. Laid the foundation for later work on generalised and transmuted families.
