Theses and projects

MSc Thesis

The Sine–Fréchet G Family of Continuous Distributions

King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals · Supervisor: Dr. Emmanuel Afuecheta

The Sine–Fréchet G family is a flexible generator for continuous distributions that combines the Sine G family with the heavy-tailed Fréchet G family. It can capture highly skewed, asymmetric and heavy-tailed shapes. The thesis establishes its mathematical framework and derives its key properties, including the density, distribution function, moments and quantile function.

The family yields new sub-models such as the Sine–Fréchet exponential, Weibull, Rayleigh and Gompertz distributions. Using maximum likelihood estimation and Monte Carlo simulation, these models were evaluated on real benchmark datasets, where they gave a consistently better fit than classical distributions.

Undergraduate Project

Statistical Properties of the Lomax–Inverse Exponential Distribution

Modibbo Adama University of Technology · Supervisor: Prof. Sauta S. Abdulkadir

My undergraduate project, "Statistical Properties of the Lomax–Inverse Exponential Distribution and Its Applications to Real-Life Data", was later published in the FUDMA Journal of Sciences.

It took me through the full modelling workflow, from deriving a model's properties to estimating it and fitting it to real data, and my later papers built on that foundation.
