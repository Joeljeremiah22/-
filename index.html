02
The Sine–Fréchet G Family of Continuous Distributions
MSc Thesis, King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals · Supervisor: Dr. Emmanuel Afuecheta

The Sine–Fréchet-G family introduces a flexible generator for continuous probability distributions by combining the Sine-G family with the heavy-tailed Fréchet-G family. This integration produces a new generator capable of capturing highly skewed, asymmetric, and heavy-tailed distributional shapes. The foundational study rigorously establishes its mathematical framework and derives essential properties of the proposed family, including the PDF, CDF, moments, and quantile function. To demonstrate its practical value, the family was used to extend classical distributions, yielding specific submodels such as the Sine–Fréchet exponential, Weibull, Rayleigh, and Gompertz distributions. Using maximum likelihood estimation (MLE) and Monte Carlo simulations, the performance of these models was thoroughly evaluated using real-world benchmark datasets. The results consistently showed that the new family provides a substantially better and more accurate fit than traditional statistical distributions, offering an advanced tool for data analysis in engineering, finance, and environmental sciences.
